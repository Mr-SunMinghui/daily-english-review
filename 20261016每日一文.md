# 2026年10月16日英语复习

## A Useful Tool Needs a Watchful City

At a recent public meeting, a city **council** discussed whether artificial intelligence should help deal with residents' complaints. The proposal was not to let a machine make final decisions. Instead, it would sort a large number of messages, find repeated problems, and **expedite** simple cases such as a broken street lamp or a missed rubbish collection.

Supporters said the system could make public service more **efficient**. It could also help a small team **supervise** the use of public money. A report might show whether funding was fairly **distributed** among different areas, or whether a certain group had become a neglected **minority**. In this sense, the tool would not replace officials; it would give them a clearer **reference** for action.

Yet several speakers asked for close **scrutiny**. Data can be incomplete, and a model may **distort** a real person's situation. An **adolescent** who writes an unclear complaint, for example, should not be treated as an **idle** troublemaker. Nor should a poor district be seen as **barren** simply because its residents use less formal language online.

The meeting ended with a practical **statement**: the city would first run a short trial, publish its rules, and invite citizens to **observe** the results. This is an **advisable** approach. New technology can improve public work, but only when human judgment remains present and responsibility is not **sacrificed** for speed.

## 习题（英语二阅读风格 · 4题）

1. What is the main purpose of the proposed AI system?

   A. To make final decisions for officials.  
   B. To handle every complaint without people.  
   C. To organize complaints and speed up simple cases.  
   D. To reduce public funding in poor districts.

2. According to Paragraph 2, AI may help officials by

   A. replacing their work completely.  
   B. offering information for better decisions.  
   C. limiting messages from minority groups.  
   D. collecting money from residents.

3. Why is the adolescent mentioned?

   A. To show that online language can affect a model's judgment.  
   B. To prove young people dislike AI.  
   C. To explain why complaints are always unclear.  
   D. To suggest that teenagers should not complain.

4. The writer's attitude toward the proposal is best described as

   A. fully negative.  
   B. cautiously supportive.  
   C. uninterested.  
   D. doubtful of all public services.

### 答案与解析

1. C。首段的 *sort messages, find repeated problems, and expedite simple cases* 是直接依据；系统不是最终决策者。
2. B。第二段说它给官员更清晰的 **reference**，即为判断提供信息，而非取代官员。
3. A。该例说明输入表达不规范时，模型可能误判真实处境，呼应 **distort**。
4. B。末段肯定技术能改善工作，但要求保留人工判断和责任，属于“谨慎支持”。

## 文章解析

### 全文翻译

在最近的一次公开会议上，市议会讨论人工智能是否应协助处理居民投诉。提案并非让机器作最终决定，而是让它整理大量信息、找出反复出现的问题，并加快处理路灯损坏、垃圾漏收等简单事项。

支持者认为，该系统能让公共服务更高效，也能帮助一个小团队监督公共资金的使用。报告可以显示资金是否在不同地区公平分配，或某个少数群体是否被忽视。这样一来，工具不是替代官员，而是为其行动提供更清晰的参考。

但多位发言者要求严格审查。数据可能不完整，模型也可能歪曲一个人的真实处境。例如，书写不清楚投诉的青少年，不该被当作无所事事的麻烦制造者；贫困地区也不应仅因居民较少使用正式网络语言就被视为“贫瘠”。

会议最后形成一项务实声明：城市将先进行短期试运行，公布规则，并邀请市民观察结果。这是明智做法。新技术可以改善公共工作，但前提是人工判断仍在场，不能为了速度牺牲责任。

### 重点词汇

- **council** n. 议会；委员会。city council 市议会。
- **expedite** v. 加快，促进。expedite a case 加快案件处理。
- **efficient** adj. 高效的；**efficiency** n. 效率。
- **supervise** v. 监督，管理。supervise the use of funds 监督资金使用。
- **distribution / distribute** n./v. 分配；分布。be distributed among… 在……之间分配。
- **minority** n. 少数群体；少数。反义词 majority。
- **scrutiny** n. 严格审查。under close scrutiny 在严密审查下。
- **distort** v. 歪曲；扭曲。distort the facts 歪曲事实。
- **adolescent** n. 青少年（也可作形容词）。
- **idle** adj. 无所事事的；闲置的。an idle machine 闲置机器。
- **barren** adj. 贫瘠的；无成果的。barren land 贫瘠土地。
- **advisable** adj. 明智的，可取的。It is advisable to do… 建议做……
- **sacrifice** v./n. 牺牲。sacrifice A for B 为B牺牲A。

### 句式与阅读点

1. **not to do…, Instead, …**：不是做……，而是……。首段用来对比“机器最终决策”与“机器辅助整理”。阅读中看到 instead，要回前文找被否定的内容。
2. **whether A or B**：是否A，还是B。*whether funding was fairly distributed … or whether…* 中两个 whether 并列，都是 show 的宾语。
3. **only when + 句子**：只有当……才……。末句 *only when human judgment remains present* 限定了技术发挥积极作用的条件；这是作者核心态度。
4. **should not be treated as…**：不应被当作……。被动语态强调“人被系统如何对待”，常见于科技伦理与公共政策话题。
