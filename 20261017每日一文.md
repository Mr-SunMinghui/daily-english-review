# 2026年10月17日英语复习

## A Fair Rule for a Fast-Growing Tool

When a public **institute** introduced an AI service to answer citizens' questions, its aim was **mild**: reduce the waiting time for simple requests. The service could **alert** staff when a question appeared urgent and **arouse** attention to repeated complaints. In its first month, it seemed to **surpass** expectations.

However, quick success can hide a weak rule. Some users found that the system gave different answers to similar questions. Others were **detained** by an online form for an unnecessary second check. The problem was not the technology alone. It was the **ideology** behind its design: efficiency was treated as the only goal.

At a public meeting, one researcher argued that this view needed to be **counteracted**. A useful service should be judged not only by speed, but also by whether its rules are **ethical** and clear. If a mistake harms a citizen, the agency should not simply **blame** the user for giving incomplete information. It should examine the **contributory** causes: unclear instructions, poor data, or a rule that no one had reviewed.

The institute then published its standards and invited outside experts to test the system. This step will not remove every error, but it makes responsibility visible. New tools deserve support when they solve real problems; they also need rules strong enough to protect the people they serve.

## 习题（英语二阅读风格 · 4题）

1. What was the original purpose of the AI service?

   A. To replace public staff.  
   B. To shorten the handling time for simple questions.  
   C. To collect more personal information.  
   D. To test citizens' knowledge of technology.

2. What does the author suggest about the system's early success?

   A. It proved that the rules were fair.  
   B. It made further review unnecessary.  
   C. It might have hidden problems in its design.  
   D. It was caused by users' incomplete information.

3. According to the researcher, a good public service should

   A. put efficiency above all other goals.  
   B. make citizens responsible for every mistake.  
   C. be evaluated by both speed and fairness.  
   D. avoid using outside experts.

4. The final paragraph mainly shows that the institute

   A. has abandoned the AI service.  
   B. is trying to make its responsibility open to review.  
   C. believes technology has no risks.  
   D. will only serve expert users.

### 答案与解析

1. B。首段 *reduce the waiting time for simple requests* 直接对应“缩短简单请求的等待时间”。
2. C。第二段首句指出“quick success can hide a weak rule”，早期成功可能掩盖设计问题。
3. C。研究者认为不能只看速度，还要看规则是否 **ethical**、清晰，即效率与公平兼顾。
4. B。公开标准并邀请外部专家测试，目的是让责任可被审查，而不是放弃该服务。

## 文章解析

### 全文翻译

一家公共机构推出人工智能服务来回答市民问题时，目标很温和：缩短简单请求的等待时间。该服务能在问题显得紧急时提醒员工，也能让重复出现的投诉得到关注。第一个月，它的表现似乎超出预期。

然而，快速成功可能掩盖薄弱的规则。一些用户发现，系统对相似问题给出的答案不同；还有人因一次不必要的二次核验被在线表格卡住。问题不只在技术本身，也在其设计背后的理念：效率被当成唯一目标。

在一次公开会议上，一位研究者认为这种观点需要被抵消。一个有用的服务不应只以速度来评价，也要看规则是否合乎伦理、是否清晰。如果错误伤害了市民，机构不能只责怪用户提供的信息不完整，而应检查促成错误的因素：不清楚的说明、糟糕的数据，或无人审查的规则。

随后，该机构公布了标准，并邀请外部专家测试系统。此举不能消除每一个错误，却让责任变得可见。新工具在解决真实问题时值得支持，也需要足够强的规则来保护它服务的人。

### 重点词汇

- **institute** n. 机构；研究院。a public institute 公共机构。
- **mild** adj. 温和的；轻微的。a mild measure 温和的措施。
- **alert** v. 使警觉；提醒。alert sb to sth 提醒某人注意某事。
- **arouse** v. 唤起，引起。arouse public attention 引起公众关注。
- **surpass** v. 超过，胜过。surpass expectations 超出预期。
- **detain** v. 耽搁；拘留。be detained by… 被……耽搁。
- **ideology** n. 思想体系；意识形态。文中指设计者的价值取向。
- **counteract** v. 抵消，对抗。counteract a harmful effect 抵消有害影响。
- **ethical** adj. 合乎伦理的。ethical rules 伦理规则。
- **blame** v. 责怪。blame A for B 因B责怪A。
- **contributory** adj. 促成的，起作用的。contributory causes 促成因素。

### 句式与阅读点

1. **When + 过去式，主句过去式**：首句交代背景；主干是 *its aim was mild*，冒号后解释 aim 的具体内容。
2. **not only A, but also B**：*not only by speed, but also by whether…* 表示评价标准并列；whether 引导宾语从句。
3. **If + 现在时，should + 动词原形**：*If a mistake harms…, the agency should…* 是公共政策写作中常见的“条件—建议”结构。
4. **过去分词作后置定语**：*a rule that no one had reviewed* 中 that 引导定语从句，修饰 rule。阅读时先抓名词 rule，再看其被怎样限定。
